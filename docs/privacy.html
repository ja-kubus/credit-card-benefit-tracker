Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE]

Summary: If you choose to link an account, your bank login happens only inside Stripe's secure screen — we never see or store your banking credentials. Your transaction data is stored on your device. We don't sell your data, and you can delete it any time in the app.

Information we collect

Manual statement upload: statements you import are processed and stored on your device only. They are not sent to our servers.

If you choose to link an account (optional):

  • Sign in with Apple identifier. A stable, anonymous identifier used only to associate you with your linked-account connection. We do not collect your name or email through this flow.
  • Account linking via Stripe. We use Stripe Financial Connections. You enter your bank credentials directly into Stripe's interface — the app and our server never receive them. Stripe provides us access to your transactions (amount, description, date) and basic account metadata (institution name, last 4 digits, account type).
  • Transaction data. Transactions retrieved via Stripe are returned to the app and stored on your device. Our server does not retain them.

We do not collect your bank username/password, full account numbers, precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

How we use information

  • To display your benefits, spending, and points on your device.
  • To maintain your linked-account connection (via Stripe) so transactions can refresh.
  • We do not use your data for advertising and do not sell it.

Third-party services

  • Apple – Sign in with Apple — Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe – Financial Connections, which handles your banking credentials directly — Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • Google Cloud Run hosts our backend, which brokers requests to Stripe and stores no transaction data.

Data storage and security

  • Transaction data is stored on your device.
  • Our backend holds no database of your data; the only mapping (your app identifier to a Stripe customer) is stored within Stripe.
  • All network communication uses encryption in transit (HTTPS).
  • Access to Stripe is authenticated by a secret key held only on our server, never in the app.

Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data: removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall.
  • Disconnect an account: stops syncing and removes that account's imported transactions from your device.
  • Delete all linked data: disconnects every linked account and deletes your associated record on our server (via Stripe).

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required by your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.
