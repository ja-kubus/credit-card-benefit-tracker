Credit Card Benefit Tracker

Track your credit-card benefits, recoup annual fees, and never leave a perk unused.

Credit Card Benefit Tracker is an iOS app that helps you get the most out of your credit cards. It organizes each card's benefits by period (monthly, quarterly, annual), shows how much value you've recouped against the annual fee, breaks down your spending by category, and recommends the best card to use for each purchase.

How your transactions get in

Manual upload (free): import PDF or CSV statements. Everything is processed and stored on your device.

Automatic linking (optional, paid tiers): connect a credit card through Stripe Financial Connections to import transactions automatically. Your bank login is entered only inside Stripe's secure screen — the app never sees your banking credentials, and transactions are stored on your device.

Your privacy

  • Bank credentials are never seen or stored by us — they're handled entirely by Stripe.
  • Transaction data is stored on your device, not on our servers.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • You can disconnect an account or delete all linked data at any time in the app.
